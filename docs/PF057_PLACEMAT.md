# PF-057 — DemoBank bilingual financing placemat

## Scope

Two genuine A4 portrait brochure pages, English then Malay. This is a fictional design demonstration, **not a financial offer**, a bank statement, advice or an application. Every printed/exported page carries `SAMPLE — FICTIONAL FINANCING DEMONSTRATION` and an explicit no-offer warning. All dates, account types, income bands, amounts and rates are fictional. Contacts use the reserved `.example` domain; there are no payment QR codes or application links.

The supplied English/Malay images informed only the hierarchy: full-width hero, three benefit-style explanations, rounded information card, grouped comparison and footer. Neither reference image nor any original bank logo, photograph, font, customer identifier, registration or claim is included. This is not a pixel-perfect reproduction.

## Rate model

One basis point is 0.01 percentage point. With integer `baseBp` and `rebateBp`:

`resultBp = baseBp × (10000 − rebateBp) / 10000`

This fixture yields exactly `[600, 500, 400, 900, 750]` basis points (6.00%, 5.00%, 4.00%, 9.00%, 7.50%). The no-rebate scenarios are 7.50% and 5.50%. Fractional basis-point results, negative/unsafe integers and rebates over 100% fail explicitly rather than being silently rounded. Rebate is a percentage **of the base rate**, not a percentage-point deduction. The assumed timely-payment condition is stated on each page.

The explanatory flat-interest model is `original principal × annual flat rate × term in years`, excluding declining balances, fees and compounding. It is **not an effective rate or APR**. No monetary savings or repayment claim is made. The sample ceiling and term are illustrative inputs, not credit availability or eligibility promises.

## Physical print-safety regression

Critical fictional/no-offer warnings, rate assumptions, annual units and formula paragraphs use 10.667 CSSpx (approximately 8pt in Chromium PDF). The hero is 318px high, benefit strip 110px, explanation card 570px and footer 120.52px; all retain the real two-page PrintForm path. Original art, numeric data and bilingual wording are unchanged.

`scripts/pf057-print-safety.mjs` measures **actual PDF text transforms in physical points**, using embedded-font ascent/descent for conservative glyph bboxes (not baseline-only coordinates or CSS-size assertions). Every occurrence of each critical text must be present, at least 7.5pt, at least 5mm above the physical page bottom and at least 7mm from horizontal edges. Original PDF secondary warning: 5.4pt / 1.286mm bottom clearance. Corrected English and Malay pages: approximately 8pt / 7.156mm lowest clearance. Source and offline PDFs are both verified.

DOM Range bounds also check minimum 10px type, safety margins and internal pitch/rate/date, table/note and footer overlaps. Playwright reproduces the original 6.9pt/5.4pt and 1.3mm unsafe footer on an actual two-page PDF, then independently rejects readable warnings shifted too low. Unit tests cover exact thresholds, descenders, split ligatures, repeated text and missing copy. No production engine/default is altered. A baseline-reproduced PF056 PDF checker kerning issue (`T` + `otal ...`) is handled only inside its row-header column; negative tests retain missing/misplaced-label and wrong-amount rejection.

Non-critical table headings/sublabels remain compact at 6.75pt, including the enlarged income/rebate units (previously 4.95pt). Decorative eyebrow/page labels and reserved contacts remain approximately 5.025–6pt. They are reported separately in `pdf.pages[].safety.smallText`; none substitutes for a critical warning. At full A4 size the tables are readable but compact; zoom is recommended for fitted mobile preview.

## Licenses / print limitations

Original illustration, mark, icons and template code use the repository MIT license. The unmodified DejaVu Sans Condensed regular/bold fonts already licensed under `assets/demobank/FONT-LICENSE.txt` are reused and embedded in the standalone HTML; their license is also packaged. No proprietary font or photo is used.

Verified PDF generation uses Chromium with `preferCSSPageSize: true` and `printBackground: true`. Choose A4, portrait, 100% scale, margins none and background graphics on; disable browser headers/footers. Chromium's PDF media box may differ by a fraction of a point from exact A4 because of CSS-to-PDF quantization. The artwork reaches the edge: physical printers without borderless A4 will add margins or require scaling. This is not a commercial press-ready PDF with bleed/crop marks.

Small explanatory copy requires paper-size or zoomed reading; the 390px fitted preview intentionally retains A4 typography. Physical printer hardware, other PDF drivers, Firefox/WebKit, accessibility PDF tagging and a native Malay language review are not certified by this implementation. There is no original-photo/font match or pixel-perfect claim.
